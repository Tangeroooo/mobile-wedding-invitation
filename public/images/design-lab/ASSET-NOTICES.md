## Lettering outlines

The SVG files in `lettering/` contain fixed lettering outlines only. No original font binary from the sources below is stored in this repository.

- Black Rush by Basni.std — free for personal use. Source: https://www.dafont.com/blackrush.font
- Selima by Jroh Creative — marked 100% free by the author. Source: https://www.dafont.com/selima.font
- Brusher by Vlad Cristea — marked 100% free. Source: https://www.dafont.com/brusher.font
- Rough Brush Script by Isaac K — marked 100% free. Source: https://www.dafont.com/rough-brush-script.font
- Bretageds by Indotitas Squad — free for personal use. Source: https://www.dafont.com/bretageds.font
- Dry Brush by Levi Szekeres — free for personal and non-profit use; online personal work must link to the font source. Source: https://www.dafont.com/dry-brush.font

The lettering is used only for this personal, non-profit wedding invitation design study. Recheck the original license before any commercial reuse.
